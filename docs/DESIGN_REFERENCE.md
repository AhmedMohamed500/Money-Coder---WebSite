# Approved Design Reference

The approved homepage screenshot is stored at `docs/approved-homepage-reference.png`. The original supplied brand artwork is stored at `public/money-coder-logo-original.jpg`.

## Design lock

The implementation preserves the approved structure and visual language:

1. White navigation with logo, Arabic links, search/language controls, and consultation CTA.
2. Bright split hero with Arabic headline, dashboard laptop composition, metric cards, and compact benefits.
3. Trust/industry strip.
4. Six compact service cards.
5. Four product cards with dashboard-style previews.
6. Five-step implementation process.
7. Industry selector and navy consultation panel.
8. Testimonials and pricing shown side-by-side on desktop.
9. Closing CTA and structured footer.

Brand colors follow the supplied navy, blue, cyan, and emerald identity. The layout remains RTL for content while preserving the approved desktop composition (copy on the left, hero visual on the right).

## Brand assets

`money-coder-mark.png` and `money-coder-wordmark.png` are lossless web crops from the supplied original image. They do not redraw or alter the logo artwork; they enable the horizontal lockup visible in the approved header.

## Content integrity

- Unverified customer counts, satisfaction percentages, reviews, names, avatars, and prices are not published as facts.
- Testimonial cards explicitly state that verified content is pending.
- Pricing cards use inquiry-based labels.
- Product previews are clearly marked as illustrative UI previews. No fabricated image is represented as a live product screenshot.

## Known visual differences

- The supplied reference contains a photorealistic laptop render that was not provided as a separate asset. It is reconstructed with responsive HTML/CSS dashboard elements.
- Genuine product screenshots were not supplied. The card proportions and visual hierarchy are preserved using labeled, illustrative dashboard previews.
- The original logo source is a square stacked composition; cropped source artwork is arranged as the horizontal lockup shown in the approved header.
